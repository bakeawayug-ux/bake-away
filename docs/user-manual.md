# Bake Away user manual

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This app keeps the records of two businesses in one place: **Bake Away** (orders, customers, baking costs, profit) and **Hardware** (stock, sales, money invested). It works on the iPhone like an app and on a laptop in the browser.

## Putting it on the iPhone

1. Open the app's address in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

The Bake Away icon now sits on the Home Screen. Opened from there it fills the screen like any other app and opens even with no signal.

> On a laptop, open the same address in Chrome or Edge. To keep it handy, use the browser's **Install** button at the right end of the address bar, or bookmark it.

## Finding your way

- **Home** shows a card for Bake Away and a card for Hardware, with the main figures of each. Tap a card to go in.
- On the iPhone, each section has **tabs along the bottom**, a **More** tab for the other pages, and a round **+ button** for adding things.
- On the laptop, the pages are listed down the left, with an **Add** button at the top.
- The small logo at the top left always takes you back to Home.
- **More** also has **Switch to Hardware** / **Switch to Bake Away**, **Settings** and this manual.

## Bake Away

### Orders

Tap **+** then **New order**.

1. Type the **Customer** name. Names you have used before are offered as you type. A new name is added to Customers for you.
2. Under **What they ordered**, type or pick the item, how many, and the price of each. Picking a product from your list fills in its usual price. Use **Add another item** for more.
3. Choose the day it is **Needed on** (and the time if you know it).
4. Choose **Pick up** or **Delivery**. For a delivery you can add the place and a delivery charge.
5. If the customer pays something now, type it under **Deposit paid now**. **Half** and **Paid in full** fill it in for you.
6. Tap **Save order**.

Tap any order to open it. On the order's page you can:

- move it along: **Booked**, **Ready**, **Delivered**, or **Cancelled**;
- **Record payment** each time the customer pays. The balance is worked out for you;
- **Call** the customer, or **Send details** / **Remind** on WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with the message already written; you press Send;
- **Add a cost** for what this order cost you (see Expenses). The page then shows what the order left you.

An order whose day has passed and is not delivered shows as **Late** on the Overview.

> If a customer changes their mind, use **Cancelled** rather than Delete, so the history stays. Delete removes the order and its payments for good.

### Customers

Customers are added for you from orders. Tap one to see what they have ordered, what they have paid and what they owe. **Edit** lets you add a phone number, where they are, notes (favourite flavours, allergies) and **a day to remember** such as a birthday. The Overview reminds you two weeks before that day.

A customer with orders cannot be deleted; use **Archive** to hide them.

### Expenses

Tap **+** then **Add an expense**. Type the amount, choose the **Kind of expense** (Ingredients, Packaging, Labour, Transport, Gas and power, Equipment, Other) and the date.

- For the kind **Ingredients**, choose **Which ingredient** (flour, sugar, eggs...) and, if you like, how much you bought. This is what lets the app show your spending ingredient by ingredient.
- After a shopping trip, use **Save and add another** to enter each ingredient one after the other without closing the window.
- **For which order?** links the cost to one order. Leave it on "Not for one order" for general shopping.

### Products

**More > Products** is the list of things you bake again and again. Tap **Start with the usual list** to begin from common bakes, then tap each one to set:

- its **usual selling price** (filled in when you pick it on an order);
- its **usual cost to make one**: your best estimate of the ingredients, packaging and labour in one.

Add, rename or delete products freely. Orders already saved keep their own items and prices.

### Profit and reports

**More > Profit and reports**. Choose the period at the top (this month, last month, this year, or your own dates).

- **Money in, Spent, Profit**: what customers paid you, what you spent, and what is left.
- **Where the money went**: your spending by kind.
- **Ingredients, one by one**: what you spent on each ingredient, and how much of the money you received it took. "12% of money in" next to Eggs means 12 shillings of every 100 received went on eggs.
- **What each product earned**: how many of each product were sold, their sales, their usual cost and the estimated profit. Below it, the estimate is set beside what you really spent on ingredients. If you really spend much more than the usual costs add up to, your usual costs are too low, or ingredients are being wasted or bought ahead.
- **Best customers** and the orders of the period.
- **Take the figures with you**: download the orders, payments or expenses as a file that opens in Excel.

> Profit comes from what you sell, not from an ingredient on its own. So the app shows each ingredient as a cost (how much of your sales it eats) and each product as a profit.

### Notes

For ideas, reminders and future bookings that are not orders yet. A note can have a **day**; it then shows on the Overview when that day is near. **Pin** a note to keep it on Home. Tap the circle to mark it done.

## Hardware

### Stock

Start by adding each item you sell: **+ > Stock in > New item**, or **Stock > New item**. Give it a name, a group, the unit it is sold by, its selling price, and the number at which you want a low-stock warning. If you already have some in the shop, fill in **How many now** and **Cost each**; that is saved as opening stock.

The number in stock is never typed in again after that. It is worked out for you:

- **Stock in** adds to it;
- each **Sale** takes from it;
- **Correct the count** fixes it when the shelf does not match (damaged, lost, used in the shop).

Tap an item to see everything that happened to it.

### Stock in

**+ > Stock in** when you buy items for the shop. Choose each item, how many and the cost of each. **Paid now** starts at the full amount; tap **On credit** if you have not paid the supplier, or type what you paid. What you still owe shows on the Stock in page and on the Overview; open the entry and tap **Pay the supplier** when you pay.

Transport for bringing the stock goes under **Expenses**.

### Sales

**+ > New sale**. Choose each item and how many; the selling price is filled in and can be changed. For a walk-in customer leave the name blank. For goods taken **on credit**, type the customer's name and tap **On credit** (or type what they paid now). Open the sale later and tap **Record payment** when they pay.

If you sell more than the records show in stock, the app warns you but still saves; correct the count afterwards.

### Money invested

**More > Money invested** is for your own money: what you **put in** to start or grow the shop, and what you **take out** for yourself. It is kept apart from sales and expenses, so taking money out does not look like a business cost.

### Reports

**More > Reports**, for the period you choose:

- **Profit**: sales, less what those goods cost you, less stock lost or damaged, less expenses.
- **Cash in and out**: what came in and went out in money.
- **Where the shop stands today**: stock value, what customers owe you, what you owe suppliers, your money invested.
- **Best sellers** and expenses by kind, and files to download.

> How cost is worked out: an item's cost is the **average** of everything you have ever bought of it. Stock value is the number in stock times that average.

## Settings

- **Your details**: your name and the names of the two businesses.
- **Your records**: **Download a backup** (one file with everything), **Restore from a backup**, and **Delete all records**.
- **Lists**: change the ingredients, kinds of expense, ways of paying, item groups and units to suit you. Tap the x to remove one, or type your own and tap **Add**. Removing a name does not change records already saved with it.

## Where your records are kept

The line at the top of Home tells you:

- **Kept on this device**: the records live only in this browser on this phone or laptop. Other devices do not see them. **Download a backup often**: clearing the browser's data or losing the phone loses the records.
- **Saved to the cloud**: you are signed in, and the same records show on every device you sign in on. With no signal you can keep working; the changes are sent when the connection returns.
